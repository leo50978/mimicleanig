Kosher-friendly home cleaning.

A careful, client-led cleaning plan for households with kosher kitchens and specific routines.

Your household rules guide the checklist.

Tell us how your kitchen is organized and what products or cleaning steps you prefer. Before the visit, we’ll review the checklist with you and confirm what the team can complete.

Identify kitchen zones

Point out any separate counters, sinks, appliances or preparation areas that need distinct handling.

Set utensil boundaries

Let us know which tools and cloths may be used in each area, and which items should be left untouched.

Confirm product preferences

Share any product restrictions or preferred products before scheduling. We’ll agree on the supplies and tasks in advance.

Please note: “Kosher-friendly” describes a cleaning visit planned around the household’s instructions. Clino does not provide kosher certification, religious supervision or kashering. The client identifies the applicable household requirements and confirms the requested scope before the visit.

What can be included?

Depending on the agreed checklist, a visit can include everyday kitchen and home cleaning while respecting the boundaries you identify.

Kitchen surfaces & appliances

Wipe agreed counters, cabinet fronts, appliance exteriors and other requested surfaces using the approved products and tools.

Bathrooms & living areas

Add bathrooms, floors, dusting and common areas to the checklist so the visit covers the rest of the home too.

One-time or recurring visits

Ask about a one-time clean or a recurring schedule. We’ll confirm the tasks and timing for each visit.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a certified kosher cleaning service?

No. This is a client-directed cleaning option that follows the household checklist agreed before the visit. Clino does not certify kitchens, provide religious supervision or perform kashering.

Can I tell the team which products and tools to use?

Yes. Share product preferences, restrictions and tool boundaries before scheduling. We’ll confirm the requested supplies and cleaning scope with you in advance.

Where is kosher-friendly cleaning available?

Ask about service in Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade locations. Availability depends on your exact address, requested tasks and schedule.

Privacy information

Clino uses the Meta Pixel to measure visits from Meta ads and understand ad performance. The Pixel loads when a page opens and sends a PageView event to Meta.

Information sent to Meta

The Pixel may send the page visited, referring page, browser and device details, and ad interaction identifiers. Meta may also receive network information such as an IP address when your browser connects to its service. Clino uses the standard PageView event and does not send names, email addresses, booking details, or contact events through this Pixel.

For details about Meta’s own processing, read the Meta Privacy Policy. You can manage advertising and personalization preferences through your Meta account and browser settings.

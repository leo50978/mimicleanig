Clino Cleaning Services

Privacy information

Clino uses the Meta Pixel to measure visits from Meta ads and understand ad performance. The Pixel loads when a page opens and sends a PageView event to Meta. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, the Pixel is not loaded for that visit.

Information sent to Meta

The Pixel may send the page visited, referring page, browser and device details, and ad interaction identifiers to Meta. Meta may also receive network information such as an IP address when your browser connects to its service. Clino uses the standard PageView event and does not send names, email addresses, booking details, or contact events through this Pixel.

Clino dashboard activity metrics

The website writes daily aggregate counts and anonymous visit journeys directly to Clino’s Firebase Firestore database. A random ID is kept in session storage for the active browser tab and UTC day to connect page views, button labels/categories, and visible time. It is not used to identify a person or link activity to a name or email. The site does not store a raw IP address, exact location, full browser fingerprint, form contents, full URLs, or a long-term visitor ID. Device, referral, and timezone information are broad categories; timezone is only a rough estimate of physical location. Firestore analytics records remain until manually removed. Because this static dashboard uses no sign-in or server function, analytics counters and anonymous session events are publicly readable and are not suitable for private or personally identifying information.

For details about Meta’s own processing, read the Meta Privacy Policy. You can manage advertising and personalization preferences through your Meta account and browser settings.

Clino does not show a consent pop-up. You can use a browser that sends the Global Privacy Control signal to prevent the Meta Pixel from loading on this site.

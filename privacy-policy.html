Clino Cleaning Services

Privacy information

Clino uses the Meta Pixel to measure visits from Meta ads and understand ad performance. The Pixel loads when a page opens and sends a PageView event to Meta. Clino’s own dashboard also records site activity and approximate IP location. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, the Meta tracking library is not loaded and Clino analytics makes no requests or writes for that visit.

Information sent to Meta

The Pixel may send the page visited, referring page, browser and device details, and ad interaction identifiers to Meta. Meta may also receive network information such as an IP address when your browser connects to its service. Clino uses the standard PageView event and does not send names, email addresses, booking details, or contact events through this Pixel.

Clino dashboard activity metrics

For visitors whose browser does not send GPC, Clino makes one request per browser tab and UTC day to IPWho.is to estimate the public IP address, city, region and country. That request sends your IP address to IPWho.is, an independent provider. IP-based locations can be inaccurate and do not reveal an exact street address. Clino stores the returned IP, city, region and country with a temporary random browser-tab ID in Firebase Firestore, alongside page names, button labels/categories, device and traffic-source categories, and visible time. Clino does not use these records to determine a person’s legal identity and does not store full URLs, form contents, or a full browser fingerprint.

These Firestore analytics records are publicly readable, and the site writes analytics directly from each visitor’s browser without Firebase Authentication or a server function; the dashboard’s 1199 code is only a convenience screen. Do not submit names, emails, booking information or other private details through analytics. Records remain until manually removed. GPC-enabled browsers are not included in Clino analytics or IP geolocation. The timezone is only a broad browser setting, separate from the IP-based location estimate.

For details about Meta’s own processing, read the Meta Privacy Policy. You can manage advertising and personalization preferences through your Meta account and browser settings.

Clino does not show a consent pop-up. You can use a browser that sends the Global Privacy Control signal to prevent Meta tracking and Clino’s first-party analytics/IP lookup requests on this site. For more information about IPWho.is processing, read its privacy policy.

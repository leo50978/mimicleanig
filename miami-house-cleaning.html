Frequently asked questions

What should I include when requesting a Miami cleaning estimate?

Send the property address or neighborhood, type and size of space, requested service, preferred date and any priorities or access details. Clino can confirm service coverage and discuss the scope.

How often can I schedule home cleaning?

Ask about weekly, every-other-week and monthly visits. The right cadence depends on occupancy, pets, foot traffic and the level of upkeep you want.

Can I request a move-in or move-out clean?

Yes. Include whether the home is empty, the move date and any areas needing extra attention so the visit can be planned appropriately.
